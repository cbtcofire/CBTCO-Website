Certified backflow assembly testing, repair, and installation — plus fire protection inspection and testing — for the Polaris corridor, Lewis Center, and southern Delaware County. This is our home turf: our office is on Polaris Parkway.

Where your report goes

Your water purveyor in Lewis Center & Polaris

The Polaris area sits on a purveyor boundary. Much of Lewis Center and southern Delaware County is served by Del-Co Water, which runs its own backflow program with online test-report submission by registered testers — while properties on the Franklin County side are typically on Columbus water. Your report has to go to the purveyor that actually serves your address.

We work both systems every week. We confirm your purveyor, submit the report the way that program requires, and track your due date for next year.
