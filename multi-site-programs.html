Programs · Portfolios & National Accounts

Multi-Site Program Management

For property groups and facility maintenance programs: one tracked schedule of every device and system across your locations, batch scheduling to minimize trip charges, and reporting that matches your work order system.

Built for portfolios

Compliance at portfolio scale

Managing ten sites means ten water purveyors' deadlines, dozens of devices, and a stack of inspection frequencies that never line up on their own. Our program approach puts all of it on one calendar: every backflow assembly, sprinkler system, alarm panel, and extinguisher inventory, tracked by site with its own due date.

We work inside national account processes daily — work order numbers, NTE limits, check-in/check-out requirements, photo documentation, and portal updates are part of our standard workflow, not an exception we tolerate.

Scope

What's included

  • Full device & system inventory across all locations, built from your records or a fresh survey
  • Single compliance calendar with due dates per site and per system
  • Batch scheduling of nearby sites to reduce trip charges
  • Work order workflow: WO numbers, NTEs, check-in/out, and photo documentation per your program's requirements
  • Consolidated reporting formatted to match your invoicing or CMMS system
  • One point of contact for scheduling, escalations, and deficiency quotes portfolio-wide
Good to know

Common questions

Do you work with national facility maintenance programs?

Yes — it's a core part of our business. If your program routes work through a platform, include the WO number and NTE in the request and we'll process it through your normal workflow.

Can you take over from our previous vendor mid-year?

Yes. Send recent reports if you have them; if not, we'll survey each property, build the inventory, and set up a fresh schedule. Nothing gets skipped in the handoff.

How do you handle sites outside Central Ohio?

Contact us — we regularly coordinate coverage for regional programs and will tell you honestly what we can self-perform versus help you place.
