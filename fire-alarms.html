Fire Protection

Fire Alarm Systems

Annual functional testing of your fire alarm system — every smoke detector, pull station, and horn/strobe verified, panel communications confirmed, and results documented device by device.

Testing that covers the whole chain

Detection, notification, and the signal out

A fire alarm has three jobs: detect the fire, alert the occupants, and call for help. Annual testing exercises all three — functionally testing initiating devices, verifying notification appliances are audible and visible where required, and confirming signals reach the monitoring central station.

Every device gets its own line on the report: location, type, result. When a detector is at end of sensitivity or a strobe has failed, you know exactly which one and where.

Scope

What's included

  • Initiating devices: smoke detectors, heat detectors, pull stations, duct detectors, waterflow and tamper switches
  • Notification appliances: horns, strobes, and speakers verified for operation and coverage
  • Panel testing: batteries, power supplies, trouble conditions, and programming review
  • Central station verification — alarm, supervisory, and trouble signals confirmed received
  • Device-by-device documentation with locations and results
  • Deficiency quotes for failed or end-of-life devices
Good to know

Common questions

Do you notify the monitoring company before testing?

Always. The account goes on test before we touch anything, and we verify signal receipt with the central station as part of the test itself.

How disruptive is the test for tenants?

Notification appliances do sound during portions of the test. We schedule around your building's needs — early mornings, after hours, or floor by floor — and can provide tenant notice language in advance.

Our panel shows a trouble light nobody can explain. Can you look at it during the annual?

Yes — diagnosing standing troubles is part of a proper annual. We'll identify the cause and quote the fix rather than leaving the light glowing for another year.
