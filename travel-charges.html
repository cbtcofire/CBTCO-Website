    How it works

    Three tiers, measured one way

    Travel is billed by how far the site sits from our Polaris shop. Anything inside 45 miles is covered by the service call.

    Distance from shopInvoice line item
    Under 45 miNo travel charge
    45–75 miTravel Charge >45 Miles
    75–120 miTravel Charge >75 Miles
    Over 120 miTravel Charge >120 Miles
    • The mileage here is an estimate. It’s calculated from the center of the ZIP code, so a large or oddly shaped ZIP can read a few miles off.
    • Your invoice uses the route we actually drive. A site sitting near a tier line can land on either side of it, and this page will say so when that happens.
    • One travel charge per trip, not per assembly. If we test twelve backflow preventers at one address, travel is billed once.
    • Not sure it’s right? Ask us before the work is scheduled and we’ll confirm the tier in writing on your quote.
