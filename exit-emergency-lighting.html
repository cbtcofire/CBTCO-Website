Exit & Emergency Lighting

Annual full-duration battery load testing and repair for exit signs and emergency light units — with per-unit results so failed heads get fixed immediately, not discovered in the dark.

Small devices, big job

Ninety minutes of light when the power fails

When the building loses power during an emergency, exit signs and emergency lights are what stand between an orderly evacuation and a dark stairwell. Code requires each unit to run for a full 90 minutes on battery annually — not just flick on for the 30-second monthly check.

Batteries are the weak point: they age silently and fail exactly when loaded. Our annual test catches the units that light up fine but die at minute twelve.

Scope

What's included

  • Annual 90-minute load test of every emergency light and illuminated exit sign
  • Monthly 30-second function checks available, or a simple log for your staff to run them
  • Battery replacement for units that fail the duration test
  • Lamp, charger, and head repair or full unit replacement as needed
  • Coverage review — flagging egress paths with missing or inadequate illumination
  • Per-unit documentation with locations and results
Good to know

Common questions

Why does the annual test take 90 minutes?

Because that's the duration the code requires the batteries to actually deliver. A unit can pass the monthly 30-second check and still fail a real outage — the full-duration test is the only way to know.

Can our own staff do the monthly checks?

Yes — the monthly check is a quick button-press function test, and we'll provide a simple log sheet. The annual full-duration test and any repairs are where we come in.

Do you replace whole fixtures or just batteries?

Whichever is economical. Most failures are battery-only. When a charger or head has failed, or a unit is obsolete, we'll quote a modern LED replacement — they're cheaper to run and their batteries last longer.
