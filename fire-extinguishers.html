Fire Protection

Fire Extinguishers

Annual maintenance, tagging, recharge, and hydrostatic testing — plus new and professionally serviced extinguishers supplied, mounted, and matched to the hazard class of each area.

Service and supply

The right extinguisher, in date, on the wall

Extinguishers are the fire protection everyone walks past daily and nobody thinks about until inspection day. Code requires annual professional maintenance plus monthly visual checks — and behind that, a longer clock of six-year teardowns and 12-year hydrostatic tests as each cylinder ages.

We track your extinguisher inventory by location, so units come due on schedule instead of by surprise. And because we sell new and serviced units, a failed or missing extinguisher gets replaced on the spot instead of leaving a gap on the wall.

Scope

What's included

  • Annual maintenance and tagging of all portable extinguishers
  • Recharge service after discharge or at scheduled intervals
  • Six-year teardowns and hydrostatic testing scheduled as cylinders come due
  • New & serviced extinguisher sales, sized to the hazard classification of each area
  • Mounting and signage per placement and travel distance requirements
  • Location-tracked inventory so nothing gets missed on the walk
Good to know

Common questions

What are the monthly visual checks, and who does them?

A quick look at each unit: in place, unobstructed, gauge in the green, pin and tamper seal intact. Your own staff can do them — we'll show you what to check and provide a simple log.

Do you sell extinguishers or just service them?

Both. New and professionally serviced units, supplied and mounted. Buying from your service company means the unit arrives tagged, in date, and correct for the hazard.

How do I know what class of extinguisher each area needs?

That's part of our survey — ordinary combustibles, flammable liquids, electrical, and kitchen areas each call for different classes. We'll match what's on the wall to what the code actually requires.
