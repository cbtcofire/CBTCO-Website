Fire Protection · 24/7 Monitoring

Fire Alarm Monitoring

UL-listed central station monitoring for your fire alarm system — signals watched around the clock, dispatch coordinated, and the whole panel covered end to end when bundled with your annual testing.

Why monitoring matters

An alarm nobody hears is just noise

After hours, your fire alarm's most important audience isn't in the building. Central station monitoring is what turns a 2 AM alarm signal into a fire department dispatch — and what turns a supervisory or trouble signal into a phone call before a small problem becomes a failed system.

Bundling monitoring with your annual testing means one company is accountable for the entire chain: the devices, the panel, the communicator, and the response.

Scope

What's included

  • 24/7 UL-listed central station monitoring of alarm, supervisory, and trouble signals
  • Dispatch coordination with your local fire department
  • Call list management — the right people notified in the right order
  • Communicator options including cellular — no dependence on aging phone lines
  • Signal verification as part of every annual test
  • Account transfers from your current monitoring provider handled for you
Good to know

Common questions

Can you take over monitoring from our current provider?

Yes — monitoring transfers are routine. We handle the account setup and communicator programming, and coordinate the cutover so there's no gap in coverage.

Our building still uses phone lines for the alarm. Is that a problem?

Increasingly, yes — traditional phone lines are being retired and can fail silently. A cellular communicator is the standard modern path, and we can quote the upgrade.

What happens when a signal comes in?

Alarm signals are dispatched to the fire department per your account instructions. Supervisory and trouble signals trigger calls to your contact list so issues get addressed before they compromise the system.
