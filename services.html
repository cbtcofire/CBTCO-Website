Inspection, testing & maintenance

Every service below ends the same way: a completed test, a tagged system, and a report in your hands. Frequencies listed are code minimums — your AHJ or water purveyor may require more.

Backflow Prevention

Backflow testing & repair

Ohio requires testable backflow prevention assemblies to be tested at least annually by a certified tester, with results reported to your water purveyor. We test, we file, and we track your due dates so you don't have to.

Annual Backflow Assembly Testing

Certified testing of reduced pressure zone (RPZ), double check (DCVA), and pressure vacuum breaker (PVB) assemblies on domestic, irrigation, and fire lines. Includes gauge-verified test results and submission of passing reports to your water purveyor.

Backflow Repair & Rebuild

When an assembly fails its test, we diagnose on the spot. Most failures are rebuilt with manufacturer repair kits — check valves, relief valve seats, springs, and diaphragms — then retested and re-filed the same visit whenever parts are on hand.

Full details →

New Assembly Installation & Replacement

Installation of new backflow preventers for new service lines, irrigation systems, and replacements for assemblies beyond economical repair, sized and selected for the hazard level of the application.

What to expect

How a service visit works

  • Scheduling. We confirm the visit window in advance and coordinate access — roof hatches, riser rooms, locked mechanical spaces, tenant notice if needed.
  • On-site testing. Technicians test to the applicable standard, tag the equipment, and note any deficiencies with photos.
  • Reporting. You receive the inspection or test report for your records. Backflow results are filed with your water purveyor on your behalf.
  • Deficiency follow-up. If something fails, you get a clear written scope and quote for the repair — no surprise work performed without approval.
  • Next due date. We log your next test date and reach out before it comes due.
