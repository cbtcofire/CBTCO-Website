Backflow Prevention

Annual Backflow Testing

Ohio requires every testable backflow prevention assembly to be tested at least once a year by a certified tester — and the results reported to your water purveyor. We test, we file, and we track your due date for next year.

Why it's required

Protecting the water everyone drinks

A backflow preventer is a one-way gate between your property's plumbing and the public water supply. If pressure ever reverses — a main break, a big hydrant draw, a pump failure — that assembly is the only thing keeping irrigation water, fire line water, or process water from being pulled back into the drinking supply.

Because the assemblies are mechanical, they wear out. Annual testing with calibrated gauges verifies each check valve and relief valve still holds — and Ohio law makes that test the property owner's responsibility.

Scope

What's included

  • Certified testing of RPZ, double check (DCVA), and pressure vacuum breaker (PVB) assemblies on domestic, irrigation, and fire lines
  • Calibrated gauge readings recorded for every check valve and relief valve
  • Passing reports filed with your water purveyor on your behalf
  • Tagging of each tested assembly with tester and date
  • On-the-spot diagnosis if a device fails, with a written repair quote
  • Due-date tracking — we reach out before next year's test is due
Good to know

Common questions

How long does a test take?

Most single assemblies take 15–30 minutes. Testing requires briefly closing the shutoff valves on the assembly, which interrupts flow through that line for a few minutes — we coordinate timing if that matters for your operation.

I got a notice letter from the water department. What now?

Email us a photo of the letter. It lists the devices on record and your deadline — we schedule the test, file the passing report, and you're done. Don't sit on notice letters; missed deadlines can lead to water service interruption.

Do fire line backflows need tested too?

Yes. The backflow preventer on your fire sprinkler service is subject to the same annual test requirement. Since we're a fire protection company too, we can test it alongside your sprinkler inspection in a single visit.
