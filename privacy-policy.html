Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 5/9/2025 · Last Updated: 5/9/2025

Columbus Backflow Testing Company & Fire ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This SMS Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when you opt in to receive SMS (text) messages from us.

1. Information We Collect

When you register to receive SMS communications from us, we may collect the following:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your name (if provided)
  • Date, time, and method of opt-in
  • Any SMS responses you send us

2. Website Analytics and Cookies

Our website uses two analytics services to understand how visitors find and use the site, so we can make it more useful:

  • Google Analytics 4 — reports on pages viewed, how visitors arrived (search, social, direct), general location at the city level, device type, and which pages lead to contact requests.
  • Microsoft Clarity — records anonymized page interactions such as scrolling, clicks, and navigation between pages, which helps us find confusing layouts and broken elements.

These services use cookies and similar technologies and collect a truncated IP address. Neither is used to identify you personally, and Microsoft Clarity automatically masks text entered into form fields. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

You can block these services by using your browser's privacy settings or a tracking-blocker extension, or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Blocking them does not affect your ability to use any part of this site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through SMS registration to:

  • Send reminders and notifications about your backflow testing appointments, fire system inspections, or service updates
  • Respond to service-related inquiries
  • Deliver occasional promotional offers (only with your consent)
  • Maintain communication records for quality assurance and compliance

4. Consent and Opting Out

By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to receive SMS messages from Columbus Backflow Testing Company & Fire. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier.

Opt-Out: You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may also text HELP for assistance.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your mobile number or any related information. Your data may be shared with trusted third-party providers who help deliver our messaging services, but only for that purpose. We may also disclose your information if required to comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Security

We take commercially reasonable steps to safeguard your SMS data. However, please note that no method of transmission over mobile networks or the internet is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

You may have rights under applicable privacy laws, including:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request corrections or deletions
  • The right to withdraw your consent to receive messages at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this SMS Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on our website with a revised effective date.

9. Contact Us

You can text us at (614) 654-3569. By texting this number, you agree to receive SMS messages from Columbus Backflow Testing Company & Fire related to external conversation. You can reply "STOP" at any time to opt out. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. Text "HELP" for assistance. Learn more in our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms and Conditions.
