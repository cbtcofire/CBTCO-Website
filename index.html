Know your inspection frequencies

The most common question we get: "How often does this need tested?" Here's the short answer. Your local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) or water purveyor may require more.

Typical inspection & testing frequencies — commercial properties, Ohio
System Frequency
Backflow preventer assemblies Annually (minimum)
Fire sprinkler systems Quarterly & annually
Fire alarm systems Annually
Fire extinguishers Annually + monthly visual
Kitchen hood suppression Semi-annually
Exit & emergency lighting Annually + monthly function test
Private fire hydrants Semi-annually
