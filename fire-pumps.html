Fire Protection

Fire Pumps

Annual full-flow performance testing of electric and diesel fire pumps, with results plotted against the pump's rated curve — so degradation shows up on paper before it shows up in a fire.

The heart of the system

Everything downstream depends on this one machine

In buildings where street pressure can't do the job — high-rises, warehouses, large campuses — the fire pump is what turns the sprinkler system from plumbing into protection. Annual flow testing measures the pump's actual performance at churn, rated flow, and peak flow, plotted against its factory curve.

A pump that's drifted below its curve still runs, still sounds fine — and delivers less water than the sprinkler system was designed around. The plotted results are how you catch that drift years before failure.

Scope

What's included

  • Annual flow test at churn, 100%, and 150% of rated capacity
  • Results plotted against the manufacturer's rated pump curve
  • Electric & diesel pumps, including controller and transfer switch operation
  • Diesel engine checks: batteries, fuel, cooling, and run-up
  • Churn (no-flow) testing on the periodic schedule between annuals
  • Deficiency documentation with photos and repair coordination
Good to know

Common questions

Does the flow test discharge water?

Yes — full-flow testing moves a lot of water, discharged through hose streams or a test header. We plan the discharge path with you in advance and manage it safely.

Our pump runs during its weekly churn — isn't that enough?

Churn tests prove the pump starts and runs, but only a flow test proves it delivers rated pressure at rated flow. The annual flow test is required precisely because churn can't reveal a worn impeller or a degraded driver.

What if the pump tests below its curve?

You get the plotted results showing exactly where and by how much, plus a diagnosis — worn impeller, suction issues, driver problems — and a written repair scope before anything else happens.
