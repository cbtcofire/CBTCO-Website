About us

The company behind the tag

Every tested system in Central Ohio has a tag hanging on it with a company's name. Here's why ours belongs on yours.

Who we are

A veteran & women owned business

Columbus Backflow Testing Company & Fire was built on three core values: discipline, accountability, and care. Our leadership's military background sets the standard for how we operate — on time, by the book, with no shortcuts on the systems that protect people and property. And as a women owned business, we bring a perspective that puts communication, detail, and long-term client relationships at the center of every job.

We also saw a gap worth closing: the backflow preventer on a fire line and the sprinkler system behind it are one connected system, but building owners were hiring two different companies to test them, coordinating two schedules, and chasing two sets of paperwork. Our technicians handle certified backflow testing alongside fire protection inspection, testing, and maintenance — so a single visit covers requirements that would otherwise take multiple vendors.

Today we serve residential and commercial properties across the greater Columbus area — homes, retail, restaurants, offices, industrial, and multi-site portfolios — both directly and through national facility maintenance programs. Whether we're protecting your water supply from cross-contamination or keeping your fire systems up to code, the goal is simple: keep you safe and compliant with zero hassle.

How we work

What clients can count on

Certified & documented

Backflow tests performed by certified testers with calibrated gauges. Fire system work performed to the applicable standards. Every result documented and delivered.

National account fluent

Work orders, NTE limits, check-in/check-out requirements, photo documentation, and portal updates are our daily routine. If your program has a process, we follow it.

Straight answers

If a device passes, we say so. If it fails, you get photos, a plain-language explanation, and a written quote before any repair work happens. No mystery invoices.

Due-date tracking

We log every system's next test date and reach out before it comes due, so annual requirements don't depend on anyone's memory.

Local & responsive

Based in Columbus, we know the local water purveyors, fire marshals, and inspection programs across Central Ohio — and how each one wants its paperwork.

Both trades, one invoice

Combining fire and backflow work means coordinated scheduling, fewer trip charges, and one accountable contractor when a question comes up.
