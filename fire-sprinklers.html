Fire Protection

Fire Sprinkler Systems

Quarterly and annual inspection, testing, and maintenance of wet, dry, and pre-action sprinkler systems — the backbone of your building's fire protection, kept test-ready and documented.

What the code asks of you

A sprinkler system is only as good as its last test

Sprinkler systems sit idle for years and then have one job to do perfectly. The inspection cycle exists because idle systems drift: valves get closed and forgotten, gauges fail, dry pipe air pressure leaks down, obstructions build in the pipe. The quarterly and annual ITM cycle catches all of it while it's still cheap to fix.

Our reports document every device tested and every deficiency found, with photos — the paper trail your insurance carrier and fire marshal expect to see.

Scope

What's included

  • Quarterly inspections: control valves, gauges, alarm devices, and water flow alarm tests
  • Annual testing: main drain tests, antifreeze loop checks, and full system inspection
  • Dry & pre-action systems: air pressure, priming water, and trip testing on schedule
  • Monitoring coordination — account placed on test before we start, so no false dispatch
  • Deficiency documentation with photos and a written repair quote
  • Five-year internals and obstruction investigations coordinated as they come due
Good to know

Common questions

Will testing set off the fire alarm?

Flow testing activates alarm signals by design. We coordinate with your monitoring company to place the account on test first, so the fire department isn't dispatched.

What's the most common deficiency you find?

Closed or partially closed control valves, followed by gauge and alarm device failures. Both are exactly the kind of silent problem the quarterly inspection exists to catch.

Our backflow preventer is on the fire line — can you test it in the same visit?

Yes, and you should — that's the core of what we do. One visit covers the sprinkler inspection and the annual backflow test, with both reports handled.
