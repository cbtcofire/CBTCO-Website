Where your report goes

Your water purveyor in Hilliard

Hilliard's public water comes from the City of Columbus, so backflow test reports for Hilliard properties are filed with the Columbus Division of Water. We file passing reports there on your behalf and track your due date for next year.

Common questions

Who receives my Hilliard backflow test report?

The Columbus Division of Water — Columbus supplies Hilliard's water, so that's where reports are filed. We submit yours after a passing test.

Can you test the backflow on my fire line too?

Yes — and since we're a fire protection company as well, we can test it alongside your sprinkler inspection in a single visit.
